20-20-20 Eye Break — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. This extension operates entirely locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any server, and no analytics or telemetry are collected.

2. Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage APIs to persist:

  • Your custom timer settings (work minutes, rest seconds, focus distance)
  • Today's break count and daily streak
  • Active timer state (remaining seconds, start time)

All of this data is stored only on your machine and never leaves your browser.

3. Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions, all for local functionality:

  • storage — Save and restore your settings locally
  • alarms — Trigger break reminders at the configured interval
  • notifications — Show desktop notifications when a break is due

4. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks. No cookies are set. No scripts are loaded from external domains.

5. Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 13. However, since we collect no personal data whatsoever, the COPPA requirements are fully satisfied.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this policy and release a new version of the extension. You will be notified via the Chrome Web Store listing.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the Chrome Web Store review page or open an issue on GitHub.
