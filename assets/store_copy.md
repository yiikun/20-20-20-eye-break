## Full Description

**Strain your eyes from too much screen time?**

The 20-20-20 rule is a simple but powerful habit: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. ophthalmologists recommend this to reduce digital eye strain, dry eyes, and headaches.

**20-20-20 Eye Break** makes this effortless — it runs quietly in the background and reminds you with a gentle on-screen overlay and desktop notification.

### Features

- **Smart Timer** — customizable work intervals (default: 20 minutes)
- **On-Screen Overlay** — unobtrusive floating reminder with countdown
- **Desktop Notifications** — get alerted even when you're on another app
- **Sound Alert** — soft chime when break time arrives
- **Dark Mode** — easy on the eyes, day or night
- **Usage Stats** — track your daily breaks and daily streak
- **Badge Counter** — live countdown shown on the extension icon
- **Fully Local** — all data stays on your device, no accounts needed

### How It Works

1. Install the extension
2. Click the icon to open settings (customise timer length, sound, notifications)
3. Start working — the timer runs silently in the background
4. When 20 minutes are up, you'll see a floating reminder and get a notification
5. Complete the break and keep coding!

### Privacy

This extension stores all settings and statistics locally on your machine. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
